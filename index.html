About CyberSense

CyberSense is a plain-language cybersecurity blog written for business owners — not IT specialists.

Every article is written to be clear, practical, and free of jargon. The goal is simple: help you understand the threats your business faces and what you can do about them.

Topics covered

  • How hackers think and operate
  • AI and cybersecurity trends
  • Cybersecurity laws and compliance
  • Protecting remote workers
  • What to do after a data breach
  • The real cost of cyberattacks on small businesses
  • Phishing awareness
  • Password safety

For informational purposes only. Not legal or financial advice.
